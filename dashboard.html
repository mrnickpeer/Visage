Why does your browser display a permission prompt?
Modern web browsers enforce strict Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) security rules. To verify whether a public profile exists across dozens of independent websites without relaying through a centralized server, your browser requires one-time permission to send cross-origin requests directly from your device.
Prefer not to grant permission?
Standard Mode will remain completely functional! It automatically audits 22 open APIs (GitHub, HackerNews, Dev.to, Wikipedia, Bluesky, NPM, GitLab, DockerHub, etc.) and provides 1-click manual verification links for all remaining platforms. You can also revoke permissions anytime in your browser extension settings.
